## [Unreleased]

### Added

- A UI stack of our own, owning layout, drawing, input and motion end to end (Design/UIStack.md, 18)
- Reactive core: `Observable<T>`, `Computation` and `Effect`, run in three phases (model, layout, draw), earliest first, reporting a computation that keeps invalidating itself
- `UIValue<T>` for every property: a literal, a token or a binding, with variants for interaction states (Selected, Hovered, Focused, Pressed, Disabled) and states of your own (`UIState`), and two faces: layout and game code read where a value is going, drawing reads where it is drawn
- `UI.Animate`: a change and everything its layout moves spring from where they are drawn, at the speed they have, a node made and changed in one go springing from where it was put; `UITransaction` says when it has finished and whether it completed
- Velocity: every value that interpolates (numbers, vectors, colours, rects, insets, corner radii) knows the speed it moves at (`AnimatedValue.Velocity`), on a spring, set by a gesture, or moved by a scroll dragged or gliding; a spring set off on a moving value starts at its speed; a value already moving that is set directly heads there keeping its speed, and one a gesture's callbacks set lands at once, under the finger
- Gestures hand their speed on, as SwiftUI's tracksVelocity: what the change callbacks of a `Draggable` or `Pinchable` set directly is sampled, the change made as it lets go starts at that speed, and once it is over what it moved that nothing took over lands still; `TracksVelocity` off for precise controls, such as a stepped slider whose snap must not overshoot
- Tokens (`ColorToken`, `FloatToken`, `Vector2Token`, `InsetsToken`, `CornerRadiiToken`) with a value per mode
- `UIRoot`, `UINode` (Clay's layout: fit, grow, fixed and percent sizes, padding, gaps, wrapping, alignment, aspect ratio, floating against the parent, the root or another node, the safe area) and the drawn values Offset, Scale, Rotation, Opacity, ZIndex, Clips and TopLayer
- Drawables: `Box` (rounded rect with per-corner radii, gradient, border, shadow), `Label` (UI.Text's layout and Markdown, SDF and colour glyphs), `ImageView` (sprites and textures, fill, contain or cover, rounded), and `UIDrawable` and `Painter` (shapes, images, glyphs and laid-out text) for drawables of your own
- A renderer of one instanced shader drawing from structured buffers in a URP render graph pass after the camera stack, in the Game and Scene views, clipping by up to four rounded rects, with up to eight textures a draw call
- Input from the Input System: pointers (mouse, pen, every finger) hit tested against what was drawn, `IPointerHandler`, `IHoverHandler` and `IScrollHandler`, `Pressable` (with long press), `Draggable` (saying which drags it takes, `Takes`), `Pinchable` (two-finger pinch and turn), `ScrollView` (rubber band, iOS deceleration, bounce, pages, wheel, indicators; a new drag heading past an end it is at goes to a scroll view it is in that can still scroll that way; past an end a drag stretches it, or with `AtEnd` set to PassOn the rest of the drag goes to a scroll view it is in that can go on, or a `Draggable` that takes it, which hands it back as the finger turns, as a sheet and the list in it share a drag on iOS; and the wheel at an end scrolls the scroll view it is in), `UIFocus` (keyboard and gamepad navigation by drawn geometry, and a first responder that has the keys while a field is edited) and `UIInput.Back`
- `TextField` (in `TimboJimbo.UINext.TextInput`, where UI Text Input is installed): text fields drawn by the stack and typed into with the platform's own keyboard through UI Text Input's `TextInputSystem`, with caret, selection, composition, secure fields, desktop shortcuts, mouse and touch selection, scrolling single lines and multi-line fields that grow
- `UINode.AvoidsKeyboard` and `UIRoot.KeyboardHeight`: content kept above the software keyboard, following it as it slides; the field being edited scrolls itself above the keyboard
- `ListView`: long lists that make only the rows near view, reusing cells as they scroll away, rows measuring themselves, what is in view kept put as rows above it are measured
- `Presentation`: sheets, alerts and pushed or full-screen pages in a stack, each laid out as a root of its own, with a dim backdrop, modality, back to dismiss, and culling below a cover while it is drawn in place (what is below is drawn again as it is swiped aside); presenting and dismissing each make all their state changes inside one change of their own and raise `Presenting` and `Dismissing` inside it, so code joins the change, as UIKit's animate(alongsideTransition:)
- `ScrollView.ScrollTo` inside a change scrolls on the change's animation, as part of it
- `DisplayTransition`: a node shown or hidden inside `UI.Animate` fades, grows or slides in and out
- Matched geometry, as SwiftUI's matchedGeometryEffect drawn as the web's view transitions draw it: `UINode.MatchName`, and `MatchId`, which what is inside a node inherits; inside one change, a node shown with the name and id of one the change hides takes over from it, flying from where that one was drawn to its own place (shown while a namesake stays, it flies out of it); one end is a picture of the hidden node as drawn, the other the shown node, live, cross-faded as one image by summing them, so nothing behind shows through, in one rounded shape whose corners go from one end's `ClipRadii` to the other's; what is inside is fitted to the flight's width from its top, text to its height, a node's own background stretching; named parts inside a pair fly to their own places inside its shape; what springs is the flight's difference from where its destination is drawn, so a scroll or a page sliding in carries it; drawn above everything at its level, out of clips; taken back mid-flight, it turns round from where it is drawn; it sets off at the speed its source was drawn moving
- `UINode.GroupOpacity`: while not fully opaque, a node and everything inside it is drawn to an image of its own first and faded as one, so overlapping parts don't show through each other; nested groups and clips work, each image covers only what its group draws, and fully opaque it costs nothing
- `UINode.Saturation` and `Brightness`: colour filters that multiply down the tree and cost nothing (applied to every pixel drawn, no image)
- `UINode.Mask`: a texture's alpha cuts a node and everything inside it, stretched over its rect (a layer, then masked)
- `UINode.Blur`: a node and everything inside it blurred as one image (dual Kawase passes on its group image), animated
- Content cross-fades: inside `UI.Animate`, a change to a drawn value that cannot interpolate (a label's text, an image's sprite, a drawable's bool or enum) fades the node's old drawing out as the new one fades in; changed again while fading, what is drawn then fades out
- A state's enter and exit animations move everything it changes when it is set outside `UI.Animate`, token values included
- `UIBuild`, extension methods for building trees from code
- Editor: the state bar, a Scene view overlay previewing interaction states, states and modes on the selected node (`UIStatePreview`), with a readout of the frame's draw calls, primitives and layers; inspector drawers for `UIValue<T>`, `Sizing`, `Insets` and `CornerRadii`; `UISimulator` and `UICapture`, which play and capture frames outside play mode
- Demos: Circle (`Demos/Scenes/Circle.unity`) and the showcase (`Demos/Scenes/Showcase.unity`), built by `Tools/UI Next/Demos`; in Circle the photo viewer flies out of the feed and home (thrown away any way, or pinched), a chat row grows into its conversation (its avatar and name becoming the header) and collapses back, stories grow out of their rings and shrink back, and Log out grows its dialog out of its button; the showcase's Store (a grid of items growing into a dialog) and App Store (Today cards opening into stories, shrunk in place by a pull down past their top and closed back into the list, which scrolls the card into view), and a stepped slider with momentum and one without; one demo component, `SwipeToDismiss`, behind every swipe to dismiss
